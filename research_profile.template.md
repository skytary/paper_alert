## Scoring rubric

<!-- You can keep this rubric as it is or rewrite it for your work. -->

**5: Must read**: directly on one of my core topics with a strong design, or something I would cite or teach right away.

**4: High relevance**: on a core or general interest with solid methods.

**3: Moderate**: related to my areas but peripheral, or relevant with a weak design. Worth skimming.

**2: Low**: only tangentially relevant, or relevant topic but outside my region or methods.

**1: Skip**: outside my interests, or hits a negative filter.
